❓ Help & Support

Common questions — tap to expand

🗺️ How do I plan a route?
Tap the A or B buttons on the Route tab, then tap your start and destination on the map — or type an address in the search box. Once both are set, tap Plan Route.
🚐 How do I set my vehicle size?
Tap the 🚐 Set Van button at the top right. Pick your vehicle from the presets — all dimensions are pre-filled with real UK specs. Adjust height, width, length or weight if yours is different, then tap Save.
🌉 Why are bridge alerts important?
The app has a database of known low bridges across the UK. If your route passes near one that's lower than your vehicle height, you'll see an alert. Always check the Bridges tab before an unfamiliar route.
🚨 How do community reports work?
Tap the 🚨 Reports tab to see live hazard reports from other drivers — police, cameras, accidents, roadworks and more. To submit your own, select a type and tap Submit. Reports expire automatically (police 30 min, others up to 2 hrs).
🔑 Why do I need a TomTom API key?
VanNav Pro uses TomTom's routing engine to calculate HGV-safe routes that avoid low bridges, weight limits and restricted roads. The free TomTom tier covers 2,500 routes per day — plenty for personal use. Get your key at developer.tomtom.com and add it in Settings.
📍 Navigation isn't following me — why?
Make sure location permission is set to Always Allow for VanNav Pro in your phone settings. On Android, also check that battery optimisation is turned off for the app so GPS isn't interrupted.
🌙 Can I use the app at night?
Yes — tap the 🌙 button at the top to switch between night and day mode. The map tiles switch automatically too. Night mode uses a dark map that's much easier on the eyes when driving.
